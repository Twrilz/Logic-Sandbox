Keyboard & mouse shortcuts
  • Shift + Drag — select multiple parts
  • Delete — remove selected parts
  • Click a pin, then click a target pin to wire — no need to hold the button, Esc cancels
  • Click a wire — cut it
  • Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V — copy / paste
  • Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y — undo / redo
  • Hover a chip + K — view inside
